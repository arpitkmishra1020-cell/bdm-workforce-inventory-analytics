# Methodology

## Procurement variability

Monthly procurement expenditure was summarised using mean, median,
minimum, maximum, range and standard deviation. Coefficient of variation
was calculated as `CV = (Standard Deviation / Mean) × 100`.

The public financial dataset is indexed to the six-month mean,
preserving the relative monthly pattern and the CV without exposing
absolute amounts.

## Inventory concentration and ABC analysis

Positive inventory holdings were ranked by inventory value. Individual
and cumulative contributions were calculated. Categories were classified
into A, B and C groups using cumulative value thresholds of
approximately 70% and 90%.

The public dataset anonymises inventory category names and retains value
shares.

## Workforce and service relationship

Service outcomes were compared across attendance-status categories. SLA
compliance was calculated as
`SLA Met Visits / Total Service Visits × 100`.

This is descriptive evidence of association and does not establish
causality.

## Service quality

The project evaluated First-Time Fix Rate, Repeat Visit Rate, Complaint
Rate, Complaint-Free Rate and customer ratings.

## Procurement-credit association

Monthly procurement and credit values were compared using Pearson
correlation. The submitted project contains six monthly observations, so
the relationship is interpreted as preliminary descriptive evidence
rather than a conclusive statistical or causal result.
