# Limitations

-   The public repository intentionally excludes the original business
    records.
-   Financial series are indexed to protect absolute business amounts.
-   Inventory item names are anonymised.
-   Workforce and service data are represented as aggregates rather than
    raw employee/service records.
-   Procurement and credit analysis contains six monthly observations.
-   Workforce and service analysis covers April-June 2026.
-   The submitted analysis is descriptive and should not be interpreted
    as causal evidence or a definitive forecast.
